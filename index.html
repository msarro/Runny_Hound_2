Runny Hound

Go, Greyhounds! Leap through Bethlehem, the Christmas City.

The game needs a browser with canvas support.

How to play

  • Space / ↑ / W / tap: jump. Hold for a higher leap.
  • ↓ / S: duck under flying stars, or drop faster mid-air.
  • M mute all sound · N toggle music · P or Esc pause (Space or tap resumes)
  • H help, including the story behind each obstacle

Meet the obstacles

All graphics and sounds are generated in code. No image or audio files are used.
Music: In dulci jubilo (“Good Christian Men, Rejoice”), J. S. Bach, BWV 368, played like a Moravian trombone choir.
A fan-made demo inspired by the Moravian University Greyhounds; not an official university product.
